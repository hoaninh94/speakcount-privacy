## SpeakCount

**SpeakCount: Voice Counter Timer** is our voice-guided counting application designed for:
- Fitness enthusiasts
- Personal trainers
- Home workout users
- Meditation practitioners
- Anyone who needs hands-free counting

### Why SpeakCount?

We built SpeakCount because we noticed that people often lose count during workouts or meditation when they have to watch a screen. With voice counting, you can stay focused on your exercise or breathing while the app counts for you.

### Key Features

- Voice-guided counting (Text-to-Speech)
- Forward and reverse counting
- Multiple rounds support
- Rest timer between rounds
- Vibration feedback
- Dark mode
- Works offline
- No account required
- No personal data collection
