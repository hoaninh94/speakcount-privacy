## Features

### Voice Counter for Workouts
SpeakCount counts aloud so you can stay focused on your workout. Perfect for:
- Push Ups, Squats, Sit Ups, Pull Ups
- Bench Press, Dumbbell Exercises
- HIIT Workouts, Circuit Training
- Home Workouts, Gym Training

### Voice Guided Counting
- Forward counting (1 → 50)
- Reverse counting (50 → 1)
- Adjustable counting speed
- Voice feedback
- Hands-free counting

### Multi-Round Support
- Build advanced workout routines
- Multiple rounds with automatic restart
- Rest timer between rounds
- Perfect for strength training

### Breathing & Meditation
- Box breathing exercises
- Deep breathing
- Meditation sessions
- Mindfulness practice
- Focus training

### Clean Design
- Modern dark mode
- Easy routine management
- Fast setup
- No unnecessary complexity
